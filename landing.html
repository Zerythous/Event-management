Frequently Asked Questions

Everything first-year squads should confirm before entering the competition portal.

Do we need prior knowledge of Machine Learning or Python? ▾
No! AI-ATHLON is designed specifically for first-year students. You only need analytical reasoning, structured prompting, and basic 1st-year C syntax (if-else, loops, 1D arrays, strings) for Round 2.
What if my teammate has never written a line of C code? ▾
Compete together as a complementary squad! One partner handles AI prompt engineering and test-case analysis while the other applies beginner C debugging fixes.
Can we use free accounts on ChatGPT or Google Gemini? ▾
Yes! Free accounts on ChatGPT, Google Gemini, or Claude are 100% permitted. Be sure to save your conversation share link for prompt-count verification in Round 2.
What hardware do we need to bring? ▾
For Round 1, any phone, tablet, or laptop works seamlessly. From Round 2 onwards, full desktop workstations and campus internet are provided in the computer lab.
