Frequently Asked Questions

Everything first-year students need to know before the opening bell.

Do we need prior knowledge of Machine Learning or Python? ▼
No! AI-ATHLON is strictly designed for college first-year students. You only need curiosity, analytical reasoning, and basic C programming (if-else, loops, arrays, strings) for Round 2.
What if my partner has never written a line of C code? ▼
That is why you compete in duos! One partner can drive prompt crafting and error diagnosis while the other focuses on the C logic and test-case outputs.
Can we use free versions of ChatGPT or Google Gemini? ▼
Yes! Free accounts on ChatGPT, Google Gemini, or Claude are 100% permitted. Just make sure to generate and save your conversation share link for prompt verification in Round 2.
What hardware do we need to bring? ▼
For Round 1, any smartphone or tablet works smoothly. For Round 2 and onwards, you will be in the college computer lab with full desktop systems and high-speed campus internet provided.
